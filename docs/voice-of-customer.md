# Voice of customer and value proposition

Updated 2026-08-24. Sources are the supplied project docs, Claude transcript/memory, older validation wrapper, existing editor documentation, and the task brief. The supplied ChatGPT URL is login-gated, so no conversation content was recovered from it.

## Sourced findings

- Players do not need another generic pixel editor. They need a valid Minecraft identity that matches the skin they have in their head.
- Blank-canvas difficulty, incorrect Minecraft format/UV work, limited reference-image/palette workflows, weak project saving, misleading ads, and confusing power-tool interfaces are recurring pain points in the supplied research synthesis.
- Serious creators resist an “AI replaces the artist” claim. The helpful promise is an editable helper: AI can propose a starting plan, while the creator retains every pixel.
- The shipped editor already has a credible technical proof: Minecraft-specific 64 × 64 geometry, editable reference/palette workflows, clean PNG export, and 2D/3D previews.
- The older wrapper’s adult validation plan treats a complete download and completed human request as commitments; it explicitly rejects using clicks as validation.

## Product hypotheses to test

1. **Outcome language:** “Create the Minecraft skin you have in your head” will be clearer and more motivating than editor/tool language.
2. **Smart Assist:** “Turn a reference into an editable skin” will win over “AI generator,” particularly for people who want control after a fast start.
3. **Human route:** Some visitors value a personal artist brief more than a self-serve flow when the detail, story, or likeness matters.
4. **Editor route:** Players with an existing skin-making habit will respond to an intentionally Minecraft-specific editor more than a generic canvas.

## Claims requiring validation or a stronger proof path

- AI plans create a satisfying identity or faithfully capture a reference.
- Grace can fulfill a human request, respond in a stated time, or accept any quoted price. No live commission queue exists in this delivery.
- Any conversion rate, testimonial, number of users, or trust claim beyond the audited feature inventory.
- “Everything stays on device.” Local editing is browser-local; signed-in project saves—including a saved reference data URL—go to Supabase.

## Voice rules

- Speak in player language: skin, Minecraft, character, reference, outfit, pixels, make it yours.
- Lead with outcome, then make the technical proof concrete.
- Use “Smart Assist” or “AI Helper,” never a magical replacement artist.
- Keep kid/teen/parent language literal and calm. Do not promise a likeness, safety, privacy regime, turnaround, or human delivery that has not been implemented.
